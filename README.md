# Interior-Product-Dashboard
🏠 Interior Product Sales Dashboard | Power BI

◉ Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of an interior products business, enabling users to monitor sales performance, profitability, customer insights, product trends, and geographical distribution of orders. The dashboard is designed to help stakeholders make data-driven decisions by identifying high-performing products, profitable customer segments, and regional sales patterns.

◉ Key Features
➼ Revenue & Sales Tracking
Monitor Total Revenue, Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Average Order Value.
Analyze overall business performance through KPI cards and trend visuals.

➼ Product Performance Analysis
Identify top-performing interior products by sales and cost price.
Compare sales contribution across product categories such as Furniture, Lighting, and Decor.

➼ Customer Profitability Insights
Track profit generated by individual customers.
Identify high-value and low-performing customer accounts.

➼ Geographical Sales Analysis
Visualize sales distribution across cities and states using an interactive map.
Analyze order volume by region to identify market opportunities.

➼ Segment & Category Breakdown
Evaluate sales contribution by customer segments.
Compare category-wise sales performance to support inventory and marketing decisions.

➼ Interactive Filtering
Dynamic filters for City and State enable deeper exploration of business performance.
